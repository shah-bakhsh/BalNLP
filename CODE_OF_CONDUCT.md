# Community code of conduct

BalNLP welcomes respectful participation across languages, dialects, experience levels,
and backgrounds. Explain technical feedback clearly, credit others, and respect
linguistic disagreements. Ask before sharing another person's text or personal details.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, and deliberate
misrepresentation of research or contributions are unacceptable. This applies to
repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and project spaces maintained by BalNLP.

Report concerns privately to Shah Bakhsh at shahbakhshtech@gmail.com. Share only the
information needed to investigate. The maintainer will consider context and may ask
for correction, remove content, restrict participation temporarily, or ban repeated
or severe misconduct. Reports will be handled discreetly, with disclosure limited
as far as practical to investigation and platform requirements. Decisions may be
appealed by replying with additional context. No response-time guarantee is made.
